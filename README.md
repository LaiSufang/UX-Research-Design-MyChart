This is a semester-long User Experience Research and Design project that is guided by the Agile UX design lifecycle/process, iterating from Research (understanding user work and needs, identifying requirements), Designing (potential solutions or alternatives), Prototyping, and Evaluation (verifying and refining designs).
The following artifacts were included in this project:
- User Research Report, which investigates user needs and system requirements for an existing system (MyChart) by examining the users' roles, goals, capabilities, constraints, and contexts of use, etc, their behaviors (actions, workflows, strategies, errors, breakdowns, and adaptations during task performance), and their experiences (perceptions of the usability, workload, satisfaction, frustration, trust, etc.) of the chosen product/system.
- Competitive (A/B) Usability Evaluation Report, in which a comparative usability evaluation of two competing products (A/B) was conducted to analyze their respective strengths and weaknesses in order to inform future design or refinement.
- Scenario-Based Design;
- Design Sketches;
- Wireflow
